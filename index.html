04 — Shortlist 01

Minimal / Pure Sine-Wave

Core idea: Intelligence through restraint.

CleanPreciseUnobtrusive
Signature ingredients

Pure oscillators, precise pitch envelopes, clean transient shapes, extremely short feedback sounds.

Potential Matiks application

Keypad, navigation, answer feedback, lightweight confirmation.

Haptic pairing

Subtle, precisely timed taps.

A clean, precise sound system that never gets in the way of thinking.

Listening references: Windows 11 system sounds; Nintendo Switch menu feedback.

05 — Shortlist 02

Playful Tactile Electronics

Core idea: Make the interface feel like a delightful physical object.

PlayfulPhysicalSatisfying
Signature ingredients

Synth blips, toy-like percussion, springy clicks, tiny mechanical gestures.

Potential Matiks application

Puzzle manipulation, buttons, tile placement, correct answers, rewards.

Haptic pairing

Snaps, pops, short tactile impacts.

Every interaction feels playful, tangible, and satisfying.

Listening reference: Playdate UI.

06 — Shortlist 03

Organic / Ambient

Core idea: A calmer, more thoughtful atmosphere for puzzle-solving.

CalmElegantImmersive
Signature ingredients

Soft mallets, wood, gentle resonances, airy textures, restrained tonal cues.

Potential Matiks application

Puzzle transitions, focus states, completion, slow-burn progression.

Haptic pairing

Soft, rounded pulses; avoid unnecessary vibration.

A warm, immersive sound world built for concentration.

Listening reference: Monument Valley.

07 — Shortlist 04

Boutique Synth / Retro-Futurist

Core idea: A distinctive electronic identity with warmth and character.

WarmCreativePremium
Signature ingredients

Analog-style oscillators, lightly saturated synth plucks, vintage electronic percussion, precise FM accents.

Potential Matiks application

Navigation, gameplay inputs, duel countdowns, rewards, branded sonic signatures.

Haptic pairing

Crisp, rhythmic, deliberately coordinated patterns.

Retro electronic charm with a polished, modern finish.

Listening references: Teenage Engineering OP-1 and Pocket Operators.
